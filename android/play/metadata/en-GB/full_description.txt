Your courses become a summary you reread, and flashcards Micabo brings back just before you forget.

Drop in a handout, a photo of the whiteboard, a Word file or a video. Micabo writes the summary, pulls out the cards and plans your sessions. Exam coming up? Add its date: Micabo counts down the days and trains you with timed mock exams.

WHAT YOU DO
• Import a course (PDF, photos, Word, YouTube or text)
• Read the summary, then test yourself with quizzes
• Review your cards with spaced repetition
• Set an exam date
• Share a course with friends, or keep it to yourself

FOUR TABS
Home: create a new lesson.
Lessons: everything you've created.
Exams: the countdown and your mock exams.
Profile: your streak, your settings, your friends.

FREE AND PRO
Without a subscription: 1 lesson, from a topic or a pasted text, plus your first mock exam.
Micabo Pro: unlimited lessons, imports (PDF, photos, Word, audio, YouTube) and mock exams. 3-day free trial, then a weekly or yearly subscription you can cancel anytime from Google Play.
