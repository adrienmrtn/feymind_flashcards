Your courses become a summary you reread, and flashcards Micabo brings back just before you forget.

Drop in a handout, a photo of the whiteboard, a Word file or a video. Micabo writes the summary, pulls out the cards and plans your sessions. Exam coming up? The plan tightens toward that day.

WHAT YOU DO
• Import a course (PDF, photos, Word, YouTube or text)
• Read the summary, fix it, reread it
• Review your cards with spaced repetition
• Set an exam date
• Share a course with friends, or keep it to yourself

THREE TABS
Courses: everything you've imported.
Review: the cards due today, nothing else.
Profile: your streak, your totals, your friends.

FREE AND PRO
Without a subscription: 1 course, 70% of the summary, 5 cards per session.
Micabo Pro: unlimited courses and cards, full summary, exam mode. 3-day free trial, then a weekly or yearly subscription you can cancel anytime from Google Play.
